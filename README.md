## 📖 Topics Covered

1. Characteristics and generations of computers
2. Functional block diagram of a computer
3. Scanning devices and electronic card readers
4. Low-level and high-level computer languages
5. Pointing devices and graphics digitizers
6. Output systems for engineering blueprints
7. Facial and voice recognition systems
8. Accessible workstation for a visually impaired user
